Walking hazard detection

A secondary test: detect an approaching terrain hazard while the player is moving.

Expanded study: setup. Select prompts on two development routes, then evaluate three distinct hazard routes and three traversable controls. No standing-still image test substitutes for the walking experiment.

Candidate families: minecraft hole in the ground, minecraft cave opening, minecraft cliff edge. Select at most a small complementary bank using reviewed negatives. These are not frozen production prompts yet.

Measurements

Warning lead time, falls and damage, false stops per minute, stopped time and combined prompt-bank delay. Compare a fixed movement script with and without the guard from the same starts. The stopping region is fixed before evaluation.
