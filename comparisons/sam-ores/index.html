Experiment

Screen ten ore classes on development routes, then freeze the bank for four 90-second evaluation routes. Include dim light, partial exposure, held blocks, inventory icons and mining particles. Separate block-level tracks from vein-level discovery.

Teacher block positions and removal events are scorer-only. Buried ore is not a detector miss. Visible-instance discovery, false detections, tracking continuity and re-acquisition are the outcomes; mining success is not inferred from masks.
