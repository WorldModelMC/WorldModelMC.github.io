What “all mobs” means here

The first cohort covers zombie, skeleton, creeper, spider, enderman, cow, blaze, ghast, piglin and magma cube. The teacher records every visible mob, including classes outside this bank. We will report that uncovered denominator; these ten classes are not all Minecraft mobs.

Teacher IDs, positions and visibility checks are scorer-only. SAM sees video and the selected prompt bank. Nearby or fully occluded entities do not count as visible misses. Projected-box proxies will be labeled as proxies until visibility and instance masks are validated.
