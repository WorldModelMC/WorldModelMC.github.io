ROCKET-3 focus around step 350

In the earlier class-stable run, two zombies were visible at step 350, but SAM 3 supplied no accepted mask after step 346. The camera then looked upward while a zombie remained nearby. An offline check found that SAM 3 still proposed masks for the zombies; our color check rejected them because their green heads dominated the whole-mask median. The updated check looks for blue trousers in the lower half of strong masks and still rejects the held sword.

What “+ controls” means

  • Detect zombies and skeletons from the live image using text-guided SAM 3 masks.
  • Prefer the visually estimated nearest hostile mob; keep a visible target while fighting.
  • Wait briefly through detector gaps, then scan when no target is visible; prevent inventory or hotbar changes during combat.
  • ROCKET-2’s earlier tested controls also adjusted aim and attack timing. ROCKET-3’s earlier exploratory controls varied guidance and action sampling.

What the models learned

Both are goal-conditioned Minecraft action policies. ROCKET-2 learned from successful demonstrations with behavior cloning and additional alignment and visibility objectives. ROCKET-3 began with imitation learning and then used multi-task reinforcement learning.

ROCKET-3 names Approach, Break, Interact, and Hunt (melee and archery) task families. ROCKET-2 describes goal-object interactions but does not publish an equivalent exhaustive task list. Protect is our evaluation mode, not a published training category.
