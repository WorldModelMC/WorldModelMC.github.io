What went wrong in repeat 3?

Zombies stayed visible, but the detector stopped supplying accepted masks for a long stretch. One weak left-edge mask then arrived late; aiming at its center pulled the camera above the nearby mobs. The controller kept acting on that old target through the gap. The earlier lower-body color change fixed a specific mask rejection, but did not solve this broader failure.

The agent and controller use screen pixels, prior actions, and segmentation from those pixels. Health and kill counters are used only to score completed runs. Diagnostic notes.
