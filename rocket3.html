What “with controls” means

ROCKET-2: the accepted Protect preset adds camera aiming correction, attack cooldown, and weapon-slot lock around the frozen ROCKET-2 policy. SAM 2 supplies and tracks zombie targets. Protect is our stationary self-defense evaluation, not a task label learned by ROCKET-2.

ROCKET-3: the frozen finalist uses the same aiming, cooldown, and weapon controls, plus adjusted guidance and action temperature. Hunt and Protect use different settings. It is an experimental configuration, not a confirmed improvement over all references. Later exploratory follow-ups are not included in these scores.

Native: released model behavior with the minimal scenario setup and initial SAM 2 target reference. The Minecraft arena, scoring and target setup are external to the learned policy.

What the models were trained to do

Both models are goal-conditioned Minecraft action policies: given a goal image and object mask, an interaction type, and gameplay observations, they predict mouse and keyboard actions. ROCKET-2 learned from successful demonstrations using behavior cloning plus cross-view alignment and target-visibility objectives. ROCKET-3 began with imitation learning and then used multi-task reinforcement learning.

ROCKET-3’s published training task families are Approach, Break, Interact, and Hunt, with Hunt split into melee and archery. ROCKET-2’s paper describes goal-object interaction across Minecraft tasks, but does not provide a comparable exhaustive named training-task inventory. Our Hunt and Protect zombie arenas are evaluations; Protect is not an upstream training category for either model.

Setup and experiment details

All 224 confirmation recordings are available, including failures. The ROCKET-3 experimental settings did not pass every predeclared comparison; these recordings are provided for inspection, not labeled as confirmed improvements.

All recordings passed automated event/video, outcome-tail and timing checks. A sample of 64 recordings was visually inspected at start, middle, scored end and video end; not every frame was watched.
